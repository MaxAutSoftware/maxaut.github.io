## Scope
Public homepage at `index.html`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience And Job
SME owners and operational leaders evaluating practical ERP and AI modernization. They need to understand ERP as the foundation through which the business operates, see how agentic AI can provide conversational access to grounded information and connect answers to controlled action, trust MAXAUT's approach, and decide to begin a discovery conversation.

## Action And Proof
Primary action: book a discovery call. Proof currently comes from concrete workflow language and a clear delivery model; no customer, partner, certification, or quantified outcome claims are available.

## Constraints
Very fast semantic HTML and CSS with a small vanilla JavaScript enhancement layer. No framework, external font request, invented platform support, or fabricated commercial proof. Motion pauses offscreen, resolves statically for reduced-motion users, and remains responsive from mobile through wide desktop.

## Direction
The Quiet System: matte off-white, near-black typography, active negative space, and one cobalt operational signal. The hero's central metaphor appears once in its headline; all later copy uses plain operational language. Agentic AI provides a simpler way to access ERP information by asking questions in natural language, then connects grounded answers to controlled action. A full-bleed pointer-responsive terrain gives the hero an expressive technical atmosphere, with cobalt snakes bouncing through its grid while a directly manipulated operational conversation demonstrates that idea.

## Memorable Moment
A full-width digital wireframe terrain bends with pointer movement while cobalt signal snakes travel and reverse across its grid. Hovering, focusing, or tapping the operational stages immediately reveals ERP event, agent context, human control, and write-back states; below, visitors can switch processes and inspect each handoff in depth.
