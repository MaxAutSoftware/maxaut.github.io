# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

SME owners and operational leaders evaluating how to modernize the systems that run their businesses.

## Product Purpose

MAXAUT helps SMEs establish and strengthen the ERP foundation, then introduce purpose-built AI agents that let people ask operational questions in plain language, access grounded ERP information, and connect answers to decisions and handoffs. Success means easier access and smoother operations without discarding valuable systems, controls, or process knowledge.

## Positioning

MAXAUT treats ERP as the core system through which the business operates. Its ERP implementation and modernization expertise is combined with custom agentic AI that gives people conversational access to grounded records and rules, then connects the answer to decisions and action. The result is operational access rather than a disconnected chatbot or experimental layer.

## Operating Context

The work sits inside operational processes such as finance, purchasing, inventory, service delivery, approvals, reporting, and other ERP-backed workflows. Prospective clients are balancing established systems and data with pressure to adopt modern automation responsibly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Modernization of existing ERP environments
- ERP selection, configuration, and implementation
- Custom AI agents connected to ERP data and workflows
- Bring-your-own-model support for selecting an AI model or provider around security, cost, performance, and deployment requirements
- The specific ERP platforms, integrations, delivery regions, and engagement model remain open decisions

## Brand Commitments

The company name is MAXAUT. The voice should be modern, credible, precise, and understandable to SME leadership rather than framed as speculative AI hype.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer names, testimonials, quantified outcomes, certifications, partner logos, case studies, or proprietary product screenshots have been provided. Future work must not fabricate these claims.

## Product Principles

- Modernize valuable operating systems rather than promoting change for its own sake.
- Connect AI to real workflows, permissions, data, and accountability.
- Explain advanced technology in practical business terms.
- Make transformation achievable for resource-conscious SME organizations.
- Earn trust with operational specificity instead of exaggerated claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public website should follow modern accessible web practices and remain usable with keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and a wide range of screen sizes.
